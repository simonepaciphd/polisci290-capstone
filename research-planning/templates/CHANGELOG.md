# CHANGELOG: [project title]

Once SPEC, RESOURCES, and PLAN are LOCKED, any change to them is proposed here first. The agent may draft an entry. Only you approve it. Nothing in the locked files changes until you have written your decision and reason.

A change is anything that alters a deliverable, a requirement, a resource, a task, or what counts as finished. If the agent finds that a task cannot be done as planned, it stops that task and writes an entry here.

Number entries CHG-01, CHG-02, and so on. Never reuse or renumber an ID. Add new entries at the bottom.

## Entry format

### CHG-xx: [short title]
- Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
- Proposed by: [student / agent]
- What: [the change, in one or two sentences]
- Why: [what was found, e.g. "the 2004 file has no county codes"]
- Affected items: [OUT-xx, R-xx, RES-xx, T-xx, A-xx]
- Alternatives: [other options and what each would change]
- Student decision and reason: [written by the student; "pending" until then]
- Files updated: [e.g. docs/planning/SPEC.md, docs/planning/PLAN.md, docs/decisions.md; "none yet" while pending]

## Rules
- A research choice inside a change (cases, measures, sources, models, interpretation) is also recorded in `docs/decisions.md` by you.
- "Go ahead" is not a decision. The entry needs your choice and your reason.
- Record the change in `agent-workspace/log.md` once the files are updated.
- If you use Git, commit the planning files after each approved change.
