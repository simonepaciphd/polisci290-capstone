# Research decisions — student owns this record

Write your choices and reasons yourself. An agent may add a clearly marked pending question, but must not fill in your decision, reasons, or approval. A pending question stays pending until you explicitly answer it. Do not erase earlier choices; add a new entry that names the decision it replaces.

Keep your own drafts and research notes in this `docs/` folder too.
