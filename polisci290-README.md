This folder has everything you need to plan and write your capstone project:

- **one overview** of what the project is and how it is graded;

## How each workflow is built

Every workflow has the same parts:

1. **Goal**: what this week is for.
2. **You produce**: the concrete output to submit, or to take to the next week.
3. **Steps**, each tagged by who does it:
   - **You**: work you do yourself.
   - **With AI (optional)**: back-and-forth with your AI tool, with a ready-made prompt at that step. Every AI step is optional: you can always do it yourself instead.
   - **You decide**: the choices that are yours to make, often after an AI step.
4. **Check before moving on**: a short self-test.
5. **Common traps**.

**The workflows are a suggestion, not a requirement.** They show one good way through each week. If a different order, or a different mix of your own work and AI, fits your project better, work out your own. You can even hand this guide to your AI tool and ask it to make a version that follows your own project plan, in the same format. The milestones, deadlines, and lengths stay the same either way.

## Using these files with your AI tool

These files are written so you can **hand them to an AI model** (whichever frontier model you have through Stanford, or the Stanford AI Playground).

1. Open a new chat for each week's milestone.
2. Paste in, or attach, that week's workflow file and your own work so far: your previous milestones and any feedback you got.
3. At each **With AI** step, paste that step's prompt.
4. Work through it with the AI. Make the decisions yourself.
5. Keep a copy of the chat. You'll need it for your AI-use note.

**What the AI does in these prompts.** It questions you, suggests options for you to choose from, gives you feedback, and can help with working material such as code and data cleaning. **The text you submit is yours.** In this course, all assignments must be written in your own words: submitting AI-written text as your own violates the Stanford Honor Code. See `docs/polisci290-ai-use-and-disclosure.md` for the exact rules.
