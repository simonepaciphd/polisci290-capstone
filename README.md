# POLISCI 290 Capstone Project Guide

Student guide for the POLISCI 290 Capstone Project at Stanford University, Fall 2026 (instructor: Simone Paci).

It walks you through the capstone week by week: one workflow per milestone, from the working thesis to the final paper, plus the progress reports, the final research presentation, the capstone formats, rubrics, and the course's AI-use rules. Each workflow step is tagged **You**, **With AI (optional)**, or **You decide**, and the AI steps come with ready-made prompts.

## Start here

- **Read it as a web page:** <https://simonepaciphd.github.io/polisci290-capstone/>. Your checklist ticks are saved in your own browser.
- **Read it as Markdown:** start with [`polisci290-00-project-overview.md`](polisci290-00-project-overview.md), then [`polisci290-README.md`](polisci290-README.md).
- **Hand it to your AI tool:** download [`polisci290-capstone-package.zip`](polisci290-capstone-package.zip), or attach the workflow file for the week you're working on.
